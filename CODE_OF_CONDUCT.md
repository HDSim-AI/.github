# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want HDSim to be a place where anyone can contribute a decision domain, a survey loader or an
evaluation without having to be already inside academic transportation research. We pledge to make
participation free of harassment for everyone, regardless of experience, seniority, discipline,
gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, ethnicity, nationality,
age, or religion.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Ask the question. Plenty here is not documented yet.
- Assume the other person read the code and reached a different conclusion, not that they did not
  read it.
- Say what you measured. "This is wrong" is less useful than "I ran X and got Y".
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that is correct but out of scope.

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, or dismissing someone's competence rather than their
  argument.
- Publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Research conduct

This project produces numbers that people cite. Two additional expectations:

- Do not report a result you have not run, and say which model and version produced it.
- Do not commit survey microdata. Most household surveys carry redistribution terms.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions, and when
representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **mustafasameen@ufl.edu**.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code, and may restrict participation
for behaviour they consider inappropriate.
